Problem Statement / Hypothesis	"""Indian mid-market enterprises, high-growth startups, and regulated fiduciaries (FinTech, InsurTech, HealthTech) face a high-stakes operational wall following the enforcement of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and strict CERT-In cybersecurity mandates. Non-compliance now carries catastrophic financial penalties up to ₹250 crore, creating an immediate boardroom crisis. Yet, achieving and maintaining compliance remains deeply broken across two core areas:

1. The Incumbent & Middle-Market Friction (The Enterprise Gap)

• The High-Cost Incumbent Trap: Enterprise compliance tools (e.g., OneTrust, Securiti.ai) treat Indian regulations as a secondary localization patch bolted onto a generic global engine. Their platforms require multi-million rupee contracts and months of configuration, completely shutting out the underserved mid-market.
• The Manual Consulting Bottleneck: Budget-constrained mid-market companies default to hiring boutique consultants or Big 4 firms for expensive, point-in-time DPDP readiness assessments. These manual audits are static spreadsheets that become outdated the moment an engineer alters a cloud configuration.

2. The Operational & Technical Friction (IT & Cloud SecOps)

• Policy-to-Code Disconnect: Legacy solutions provide high-level text ""policy guidance"" but leave DevOps teams blind to how their live cloud configurations actually match the law. If an LLM or consultant hallucinates a wrong rule interpretation, it shifts severe legal liability onto the company.
• Configuration Drift & Sovereignty Violations: Engineering teams lack continuous, automated visibility into whether their multi-cloud (AWS/Azure) environments are violating strict local mandates, such as the DPDP data localization frameworks, CERT-In's 6-hour incident logging requirements, or sector-specific RBI Master Directions.

As a result, companies are trapped between unaffordable legacy software and risky manual audits—leaving their infrastructure exposed to severe regulatory penalties and configuration drift."""
