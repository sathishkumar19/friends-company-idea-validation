| Short Idea                     | A Scrap Collection App, Where Customers can send Old items to scrap collection agent and get return of used items inform of Reward Points or Cash                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Problem Statement / Hypothesis | Urban households lack a convenient, rewarding, and trusted way to clear out accumulated household clutter.<br><br>Items like old electronics, used clothes, cardboard boxes, and toys frequently sit idle for months or years, occupying valuable living space.<br><br>C2B<br>Homeowners face clear friction points when trying to dispose of them:<br><br>• Inconvenience: Finding dedicated recyclers, driving to drop-off centers, or negotiating with unverified local scrap dealers is time-consuming.<br>• Emotional Detachment & Waste Anxiety: Owners often hesitate to throw items in the trash because they want them to be safely disposed of, repurposed, or given a "second life," rather than ending up in a landfill.<br>• Lack of Incentive: Traditional disposal methods offer zero financial or promotional return for the effort required to declutter.<br><br>As a result, usable and recyclable materials remain locked in homes, creating physical clutter for consumers and breaking the circular economy loop for recyclers.<br><br>B2B<br>• For Micro-Collectors (Traditional Kabadiwalas): Independent scrap collectors operate with low visibility, unsafe logistics, and poor B2B bargaining power. They are often exploited by large-scale recyclers due to a lack of market pricing transparency and direct corporate contacts.<br>• For Demand (Industrial Recyclers): Large recycling plants suffer from irregular, contaminated, and unsegregated inflows of scrap material because they lack a direct, reliable digital pipeline to urban supply sources. |
| Value Hypothesis               | If we provide a seamless mobile platform that connects households with verified scrap collection agents—offering on-demand pickups and immediate rewards (cash or transferable reward points)—we will eliminate the friction of decluttering.<br><br>We believe consumers will actively participate if the platform solves three core emotional and practical needs:<br><br>1\. Convenience: A reliable agent comes directly to their doorstep.<br>2\. Sustainability/Guilt Relief: Transparent tracking guarantees that items are responsibly recycled or repurposed.<br>3\. Financial Incentive: Tangible rewards (cash or points) turn perceived "trash" into immediate value.<br><br>We will know this is true when we see a steady month-over-month increase in repeat pickup requests and a high volume of high-value scrap (like electronics and textiles) collected per household.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Core. Hypothesis               | 1\. Value Hypothesis (Consumer & Business Side)<br><br>If we offer an on-demand, doorstep pickup service that rewards users with cash or transferable loyalty points, then urban consumers and local businesses will actively declutter. We believe this because it replaces disposal friction with immediate convenience, gamified financial incentives, and verified ecological relief.<br><br>2\. B2B & Empowerment Hypothesis (The Collector/Kabadiwala Side)<br><br>If we provide traditional scrap collectors with a B2B platform featuring live market pricing and aggregated volume matching, we can standardise their income and optimize their logistics. By empowering them as our logistics partners rather than disrupting them, we secure a scalable, highly motivated operational workforce.<br><br>3\. Monetization Hypothesis (The Revenue Model)<br><br>If we take a transaction fee from the scrap value differential and charge industrial recyclers a premium for high-quality, pre-sorted bulk supply, we will achieve sustainable profitability. Recyclers will gladly pay a premium to eliminate their material acquisition and sorting overheads.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
