## 1. Quick Take

This isn't a tech startup. It's a **real-estate-backed leisure business**, and that's fine. Demand is proven: badminton and pickleball courts in Bengaluru and Chennai book out at peak hours, and people already pay ₹400–1,200 an hour through Playo and Hudle. The risks are different from a startup's: **off-peak utilisation, getting stuck in a lease, and recovering the build cost before the lease ends**.

The six-facility list is the biggest mistake. Each facility has different space, build-cost and staffing needs. Choose by **revenue per square foot per hour**, not by variety.

## 3. The Five Fatal Questions

**Q1: Who is the customer, and would they pay for this today?**
- **Customer 1: adults.** Working professionals aged 25–45 within 5 km, playing badminton or pickleball 2–4 times a week, mostly 6–10 PM and weekends.
- **Customer 2: kids.** Parents paying ₹2,500–4,000 a month for coaching (badminton, TT, cricket nets, dance), mostly 4–7 PM and weekend mornings.
- **Customer 3: corporate HR.** Leagues and tournaments.
- **Willingness to pay:** **yes, today.** Prices are visible on Playo and Hudle.

**Q2: Why hasn't someone built this already?**
- **Many have, and venue economics are proven.**
- **Game Theory** (Bengaluru) and **PlayAll** run multi-sport centres. **Hudle and Playo** aggregate bookings and also operate some venues. Box-cricket turfs are everywhere in Chennai and Bengaluru.
- **What fails:** sites far from housing, venues that try to do everything (many sports, each half-used), expensive builds with 20% off-peak use, and **short leases that end before payback**.
- **What's different now:** pickleball is booming and courts are scarce. That window probably lasts 2–3 years.

**Q3: What's the distribution advantage?**
- **Strong and cheap:** listing on Playo and Hudle brings demand from day one.
- **Local reach:** apartment-community WhatsApp groups and corporate HR leagues.
- **Built-in growth loop:** coaching academies (badminton, cricket, dance) bring their own students and fill off-peak hours. Parents waiting for their kids become adult players.

**Q4: Can this be a big business, or is it a feature?**

**Illustrative maths: 4 badminton + 3 pickleball + 2 TT + 1 dance room, about 15,000 sq ft built on 20,000 sq ft leased:**

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Build cost | ₹70L–1 Cr |
| Courts: 7 courts × about ₹1L/month (₹550/hr avg × 40% utilisation × 16 hrs) | about ₹7L/month |
| TT plus dance room rental and coaching share | about ₹0.8–1.2L/month |
| **Revenue** | **about ₹8L/month** |
| Costs: lease, staff, power, upkeep | ₹3–4L/month |
| Net | about ₹4L/month |
| **Payback** | **about 2–2.5 years** |

- **5-year vs 10-year lease:** with a 2–2.5 year payback, a **5-year lease leaves only about 2.5 years of profit and gives the landlord leverage at renewal.** Go for **9–10 years**, with a 3-year lock-in for *you* only, and rent increases capped at 5% a year.
- **Cricket:** a box-cricket turf on the same land roughly doubles the land requirement. Model it separately, and add it only if the land is cheap per sq ft (outskirts).
- **$1M (about ₹8.4 Cr) yearly revenue** needs about 8–10 venues. That's a portfolio business, possible with a repeatable site playbook.

**Q5: Can the founder actually build this?**
- **Capital:** yes, from the group's accumulated money.
- **Gaps:** venue operations, lease negotiation, construction management, and coaching partnerships.
- **The first hire is the problem:** a **venue general manager** who has run a badminton or pickleball centre and brings coach relationships. Without one, the founders become full-time caretakers.

## 5. Validation Experiments

**Experiment 1: Are nearby courts actually full?**
- **Test:** Is there unmet peak and off-peak demand within 5 km of a candidate site?
- **How:**
  1. Track slot availability for every badminton, pickleball and box-cricket venue within 5 km on Playo and Hudle, at 4 time bands, daily for 14 days.
- **Success:** Peak slots are at least 85% sold out **and** off-peak is at least 30%.
- **Time and cost:** 2 weeks, ₹0.

**Experiment 2: Will coaches commit off-peak hours?**
- **Test:** Will coaching academies and instructors commit to off-peak hours?
- **How:**
  1. Approach 10 badminton, cricket, dance and TT coaches near the site.
  2. Offer fixed off-peak slots at a 30% discount for a 12-month signed commitment.
- **Success:** At least 3 letters of intent covering at least 25 hours a week of off-peak use.
- **Time and cost:** 2 weeks, about ₹2,000.

**Experiment 3: Do real lease and build costs work?**
- **Test:** Do real lease and build quotes give payback within 30% of the lease term?
- **How:**
  1. Get 3 land or lease quotes (15,000–25,000 sq ft) and 2 builder quotes (shed, courts, flooring).
  2. Model the core mix (badminton, pickleball, TT, dance) and the core-plus-cricket mix separately.
- **Success:** Payback within 3 years on a lease of at least 9 years, with a renewal option.
- **Time and cost:** 2 weeks, about ₹3,000.

## 6. Verdict: Build It (as a Business, Not a Startup)

This is the most *bankable* idea in the set. Demand is proven, it's cash-flow positive within months, and it puts the group's capital to work.

**Launch mix:**
- **Phase 1:** pickleball, badminton, TT and 1–2 activity rooms.
- **Phase 2:** cricket nets, only if land allows.
- **Phase 3:** 10m air-rifle range, maybe.
- **Never:** firearms shooting.

**Lease:** 9–10 years, not 5.

**This week:** start Experiment 1 for 2 candidate locations.
