> **Founder profile used for this assessment:** Infra/Cloud Architects (datacenter, cloud, software, DevOps). Budget about ₹30L, to be spent in stages. Limited sales experience, willing to learn. Founders based in Bangalore, Australia and the US.

---

## 1. Bottom Line

Claude's validation rated this the most *bankable* idea: demand is proven and it can make money within months. The founder criteria change the answer. The Claude validation estimates a ₹70L–1 Cr build cost for a mid-sized venue, more than double the ₹30L budget. It can't be spent in stages: the lease and the build are committed on day one. It doesn't use cloud or infrastructure skills, it isn't AI-native, and it needs someone on site every day. The Australia and US founders couldn't contribute beyond money.

## 2. Founder-Criteria Scorecard

| Criterion | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain Fit** | 1 | Real estate, construction, venue operations and coaching partnerships. A booking system or smart access control is the only technical piece, and Playo and Hudle already provide booking. |
| **Implementability** | 2 | You can start smaller (2–3 pickleball courts in about 3,000 sq ft), but each step is a lump of capex plus a lease, not an experiment you can reverse. |
| **Budget Scalability** | 1 | A mid-sized venue costs ₹70L–1 Cr. A small 2–3 court version would still take ₹20–30L, which is most or all of the budget, locked into one site. Expanding means another full venue. |
| **AI-Native Potential** | 1 | Dynamic pricing for off-peak slots and automated access are minor additions. The business is courts and people. |
| **Target Market** | 4 | Clear and proven: working adults 25–45 within 5 km, kids' coaching, corporate leagues. Easy to reach through Playo, Hudle and community WhatsApp groups. |
| **Competitive Landscape** | 3 | Local competition only. Pickleball courts are in short supply for perhaps 2–3 years. Many venues fail from bad location, too many sports or short leases. |
| **Remote Collaboration** | 1 | Daily operations, staff, maintenance and coaches all need someone on site. Only the Bangalore founder(s) can run it. |
| **MOAT** | 2 | A well-run venue with a 9–10 year lease and a loyal coaching community has local stickiness. Pickleball scarcity gives a 2–3 year window. No tech moat; another investor can sign a lease nearby. |
| **NorthStar / ARR** | 4 | The most predictable revenue model in the set: slot bookings, membership passes, coaching fees and corporate league contracts. North Star metric: court utilisation rate (target 70%+ during peak hours). ARR is strong once the venue is operational, but requires full capex upfront. |
| **Total** | **19/45** | **Poor fit for this team's goals** |

## 4. Budget View

| Option | Approx. capex | Share of ₹30L | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-size venue (Claude validation example: 4 badminton, 3 pickleball, 2 TT, 1 dance room) | ₹70L–1 Cr | 230–330% | Needs outside money or a loan |
| Small pickleball-only venue (2–3 courts) | ₹20–30L | 65–100% | Leaves no money for anything else |
| Sublease courts inside an existing facility | ₹5–10L | 15–35% | Lowest risk; depends on finding a partner venue |

## 5. Closing the Sales Gap

Selling is easy here: booking apps bring players, and coaches bring students. This is the one idea where limited sales experience doesn't matter. It doesn't help the team build B2B tech sales skills, though.

## 6. Working Across Bangalore, Australia and the US

- **Bangalore:** everything (site, staff, coaches, maintenance).
- **Australia and US:** passive investors only.
- This doesn't match a three-founder company where everyone builds.

## 7. If the Group Still Wants It

Treat it as a **separate investment**, not the startup:
1. Fund it with separate money (or a loan), not the ₹30L startup budget.
2. Hire a venue general manager who has run a badminton or pickleball centre.
3. Follow the Claude validation's rules: pickleball and badminton first, no firearms, a 9–10 year lease, and track nearby courts on Playo and Hudle for 14 days before signing.
4. Fill in `sportsfacility.md`: it is currently empty. Add city, locality, budget and land size.

## 8. Verdict

**Not the startup.** It conflicts with four of the seven criteria (domain, budget, AI-native, remote). Keep it as a possible side investment once the main company is earning.
