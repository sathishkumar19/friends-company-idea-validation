## 5. Portfolio Recommendation

The two top-scoring ideas are **complementary, not competing**:

- **#3 RegTech is the venture bet.** It needs little capital and uses the team's skills, with a deadline-driven market through 2027. Start Experiment 1 (pick one regulator) and Experiment 3 (sell a manual ₹75k gap report) this week.
- **#7 Sports Facility is the asset bet.** It uses the group's accumulated capital for steady cash flow, but **only with a 9–10 year lease and a hired venue GM**. Start Experiment 1 (14-day Playo/Hudle occupancy tracking) for 2 candidate sites now, before committing any money.
- **#5 IT/OT is worth one cheap experiment** (10 energy meters in 5 shops, about ₹15k) before deciding.
- **#6 is a pivot candidate:** test the clinic queue version only if someone in the group has clinic connections.
- **Drop #1, #2 and #4** in their current form. The adjacent ideas (corporate IT asset disposal, agri-drone compliance, college accreditation tooling) can go into the backlog. Corporate IT asset disposal fits well alongside #3, since data-erasure certificates are DPDP compliance evidence.

## 6. Pattern Across All Pitches

Most of the B2B pitches (#2, #3, #4, #5, #6) use the same template: *"a neutral, multi-tenant platform with 3 packages/modules/streams, adopted aggressively"*.

**Every idea is better as one package, sold to one named buyer, for one painful trigger.** Before the next round, rewrite each surviving idea as one sentence:

> "**[Job title] at [company type]** will pay **₹X/month** for **[one outcome]** because **[deadline or pain]** forces them to act now."
